About the resort

Easy days, early nights, and nobody checking the time

Solenne Bay Resort sits on the sheltered side of Castellan Key, where the bay is calm enough for little swimmers and clear enough for snorkeling off the beach. The resort opened in 1989 as a cluster of thirty cottages and has grown, slowly, into 180 rooms spread across low buildings under the palms. Nothing here is taller than the trees.

Mornings start with breakfast on the terrace, which is included for direct bookers. From there the day is yours. The kids club takes the four-to-twelves from nine, the infinity pool stays quiet, the spa books up by lunchtime, and the beach hut hands out paddleboards, snorkels and sandcastle buckets until the sun goes down. Dinner is at Palma, our open-air restaurant, or at the poolside grill if nobody wants to change out of their swimsuit.

Key West is forty minutes down the Overseas Highway, Miami is three hours, and the airport shuttle meets every flight into Key West International. Book direct and we include breakfast, free cancellation up to 48 hours out, and a room upgrade when one is free on the day.
